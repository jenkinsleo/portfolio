About Me

I’ve always been someone who likes making things. When I was younger it was random projects, little experiments, videos, small businesses with friends, anything that let me build something from nothing. I grew up in Calgary so most of my life outside of work and school has been spent around mountains, driving out to Kananaskis, hanging out with friends, and trying to squeeze in time outdoors whenever I can. I like road trips, late night work sessions, and finding ways to make ideas feel real. I’ve always leaned toward design, tech, and anything that feels a bit entrepreneurial, even before I knew what any of those things actually meant.

As I got older, that turned into a weird mix of interests. brand design, web layouts, marketing psychology, fast websites, cleaner systems, and honestly just making things that look good and work properly. I love good typography, quiet design, and anything that feels intentional. I also like understanding how things function behind the scenes. it’s satisfying to build something that feels simple to the user but has a full engine running underneath it.

I’m in GBDA at the University of Waterloo now, and most of what I do there connects back to the stuff I already loved building. I get to study design, tech, UX, media, business, and use every project as an excuse to create something real instead of just completing an assignment.
